Before a single ballot is cast, the map itself moves.

During the 116th Congress, North Carolina was divided into 13 House districts. As you scroll, the census caliper sweeps from the Blue Ridge Mountains toward the Atlantic coast.

One census adds a fourteenth seat and redraws the Piedmont.

Following the 2020 Census, North Carolina gained a 14th congressional seat. Subsequent 2023 boundary lines reshaped Districts 6, 13, and 14 across Durham, Wake, Guilford, and Mecklenburg counties.
